1. When developing the tiling controller:

Your tile_controller already has a known inefficiency where ACCUMULATE burns 63 idle cycles doing nothing (it's in your CLAUDE.md backlog) — so this design is already state-heavy and not latency-optimized, and one more single-cycle wait state per address fetch is negligible next to that. Pipelining the address generation to hide the latency is a real optimization, but it adds real complexity (you have to track "which cycle's data is this" as it flows through, especially once you're juggling multiple matmuls in sequence — Q, K, V, scores, etc.), and correctness bugs from getting that wrong are exactly the kind of thing that's hard to debug on hardware. Get the whole attention pipeline working correctly with simple wait-states first; if you profile it later and BRAM latency turns out to actually be your bottleneck (unlikely — your softmax and matmul stages are probably far more expensive), you can revisit and pipeline it then.
